Who we are

Climbing routes don't maintain themselves — and neither does representation.

Bolts corrode, anchors wear out, and routes need rebolting to stay safe. That technical work has long been done almost entirely by men. Rebolting Hearts exists to change that: we train women in rebolting and bolting, give them the visibility and confidence to take on this work in their own climbing communities, and connect them with a wider network of women doing the same across Europe and Morocco.

👁️ Visibility

We highlight the women already rebolting and bolting routes, and make the skill visible as something any woman climber can learn — not a niche reserved for a few.

🛠️ Workshops

Hands-on, technical workshops covering anchor inspection, hardware, drilling, route gardening and bolting ethics — taught by and for women.

🤝 Support

A network of women rebolters across Europe and Morocco sharing tools, local access, mentorship, and route-maintenance projects.
