What your support makes possible

More sustainable climbing

Maintaining existing climbing areas reduces the pressure to develop new natural sites.

Women's access to technical skills

A dedicated space for women to gain technical knowledge and practical experience in a field where access to training can be limited.

Knowledge sharing

Skills learned at the camp are shared across the local climbing community, with impact well beyond the event itself.

Safer climbing infrastructure

Rebolting ageing routes keeps climbing infrastructure in good shape and makes crags safer for everyone.

International connections

Climbers from different countries and communities brought together around shared knowledge, skills and environmental responsibility.

Become a partner

We're looking for partners who want to support women's technical skills, sustainable climbing practices, and the maintenance of existing climbing areas. You can support:

  • Equipment and training kits
  • Rebolting materials
  • Professional instruction
  • Transportation and logistics
  • The project as a whole

Not just financial

We're also keen on equipment sponsorships and in-kind partnerships with climbing and outdoor companies. Every contribution goes directly into the training and into technical skills that stay in the Moroccan climbing community.
