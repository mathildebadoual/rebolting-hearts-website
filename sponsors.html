Become a sponsor

Whether you're a climbing brand, a gear manufacturer, or a local business, your support directly funds bolting hardware, travel for workshops, and training for women across Europe and Morocco. Here's how sponsorship works:

Trail

For individuals and small local shops.

  • Logo on our website
  • Shoutout on Instagram
  • Thank-you on event day
Get in touch

Anchor

For brands supporting a full workshop.

  • Everything in Trail
  • Logo featured on workshop page
  • Mentioned in event recap & photos
  • Product seeding opportunities
Get in touch

Summit

For long-term partners of the mission.

  • Everything in Anchor
  • Named partner across the season
  • Co-branded workshop content
  • Direct input on where we expand next
Get in touch

Tiers and benefits are a starting point — happy to tailor a partnership to what makes sense for you. Reach out at contact@reboltinghearts.org or via our contact form.
